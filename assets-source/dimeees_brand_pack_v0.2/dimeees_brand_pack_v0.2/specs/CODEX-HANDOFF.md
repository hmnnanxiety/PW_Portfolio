When implementing the Hero:

1. Treat `brand/references/dimeees-identity-board-v1.png` as visual reference, not a production image.
2. Render `dimeees` as Eudoxus Sans HTML text.
3. Use the center-left Logo Lockup as composition inspiration.
4. Use the lower-left standalone wordmark as accent/typography inspiration.
5. Keep the hero head as an independent transparent image layer.
6. The supplied standalone heads are candidates only; do not assume either is a final master without review.
7. Keep accent SVGs independently positioned and responsive.
8. Preserve the existing page IA and do not begin Motion.
9. Avoid generic image-card treatment for the Hero artwork.
10. Do not add project content during the Hero pass.
