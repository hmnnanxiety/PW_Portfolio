# Hero Art Direction

## Goal

The Hero must immediately communicate that `dimeees` is a personal creative-technology identity rather than a generic portfolio template.

## Composition

Preferred hierarchy:

1. small positioning label: `VISUALS × INTERFACES × SOFTWARE`
2. expressive brand lockup: head + huge `dimeees`
3. formal name: `Dimas Satria Widjatmiko`
4. short positioning copy
5. primary/secondary CTA

The head and wordmark should visually interact. Avoid the generic two-column pattern where artwork sits isolated inside a rectangular card.

## Wordmark implementation

- Use Eudoxus Sans in HTML/CSS.
- Large desktop weight: 800 when available.
- Preserve accessibility and text selection.
- Use supplied SVG accents as independently positioned decorative elements.

## Hero artwork requirements

For the eventual locked head master:

- transparent background
- no wordmark text embedded
- no white/card background
- flat illustration treatment consistent with existing mascot set
- curly-hair silhouette and round glasses preserved
- suitable for approximately 420–620 CSS px on large desktop
- target source dimension: at least ~1400 px on its longest visible axis for comfortable high-density rendering
- preserve breathing room around hair; avoid tight cropping

## Responsive behavior

Desktop: integrated horizontal lockup is encouraged.
Mobile: recompose rather than simply shrink; the head may move above/beside the wordmark while preserving clear reading order.

## Do not

- upscale the small expression/action sprites into the hero
- place the hero head inside a generic gray media card
- introduce random blobs, glassmorphism, gradients, or icon clouds
- add motion before the static composition is approved
